<div class="poem stanza">years pass.
the streets grow quiet.
the closet door stands open.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">she is safe now.</div>

<aside class="note">“safe” in one stanza, taken back in the next.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza"><mark class="clue">but no one told the monsters.</mark></div>

<div class="poem stanza">they follow her into dreams,
whisper through the silence
of an otherwise peaceful life.</div>

<aside class="note">the question from part one, still unanswered.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza"><em><mark class="clue">can anybody hear me?</mark></em></div>

<div class="poem stanza">when her mother returns,
the emptiness remains,</div>

<div class="poem stanza">because sometimes
the person who broke your heart
cannot be the one
who teaches it to heal.</div>
