<div class="poem stanza">she builds a home,
a marriage,
a life that little girl
could never have imagined.</div>

<aside class="note">the children arrive as faces, not a list.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">and then she sees them.
<mark class="clue">her daughter’s face.</mark>
<mark class="clue">her son’s face.</mark></div>

<div class="poem stanza">the love she was never sure could exist
is real,
and she carries it.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">she becomes the arms
she once needed,
the voice that answers,
the promise that stays.</div>

<aside class="note">the safest moment and the trigger, side by side. no warning.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">but <mark class="clue">a slammed door</mark>
<mark class="clue">becomes a gunshot.</mark>
a raised voice
becomes a monster.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">and just like that,
she is small again,
hiding in a closet
<mark class="clue">that no longer exists.</mark></div>
