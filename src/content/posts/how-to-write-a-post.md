## The basics

Write in Markdown. Two line breaks start a new paragraph. Use `**bold**`, `*italics*`, and `[link text](https://example.com)`.

<aside class="note">this is a margin note</aside>

To make a margin note, write `<aside class="note">your scribble</aside>` on its own line. On a laptop it floats into the right margin in handwriting. On a phone it tucks under the paragraph.

To <mark>highlight like a highlighter</mark>, wrap words in `<mark>` and `</mark>`.

> A line starting with `>` becomes a big italic pull quote.

## Short stories

Put a story on the `writing-life` shelf. Use three dashes (`---`) between scenes and you get the scribble divider as your scene break. The drop cap on the first letter happens automatically.

## The occasional poem

If one sneaks in anyway, wrap it in a poem box so the line breaks stay put:

<div class="poem">
the crow keeps the key
under his tongue, and the library
waits for morning
</div>

## Book reviews

Add a `book:` section to the top of the post (title, author, and an optional `rating` from 0 to 5, halves allowed) and a little catalog card appears under the headline.
