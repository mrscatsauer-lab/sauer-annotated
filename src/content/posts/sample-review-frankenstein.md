Victor Frankenstein spends two years building a person and roughly four seconds deciding he wants nothing to do with him. <mark>That is not a horror premise. That is a group chat.</mark>

<aside class="note">margin notes float out here on big screens</aside>

The creature, meanwhile, teaches himself to read by eavesdropping on a family through a hole in the wall, which is the most English-teacher thing that has ever happened in fiction.

> Beware; for I am fearless, and therefore powerful.

---

## The verdict

Half a star off for the framing letters, which are Robert Walton's personal podcast that nobody asked for. Otherwise: required reading, especially for anyone who has ever made something and then panicked about it.
