I read this one over the summer, and I'm still thinking about how cute it was.

## The setup

Evie Sage needs a job. Badly. The only one she can get is assistant to the Villain, the most feared man in the kingdom.

So she takes it. And the book becomes an office comedy, except the office belongs to a villain, the coworkers are henchmen, and the daily tasks occasionally involve things a normal assistant would not put on a résumé.

<aside class="note">imagine your boss is terrifying. now imagine he's literally named the Villain.</aside>

## The office humor

This is the best part. The office humor is hilarious. Evie is funny, the situations are funny, and the whole idea of normal workplace problems happening inside an evil lair never stopped being funny to me.

It's such a cute idea, and it works because the book commits to it.

## Rooting for the bad guys

This is the first book where I was rooting for the bad guys. Which, as it turns out, aren't exactly the bad guys.

That's the trick of it. The book calls him the Villain on every page, and the more you get to know him, the less that label fits. By the end, I was fully on Team Villain, and I didn't even feel bad about it.

<aside class="note">first time i've ever cheered for the guy with "villain" in his job title.</aside>

## Why it's four stars and not five

The ending doesn't really end. Book one stops on a cliffhanger, right in the middle of the danger, so you have to pick up the next book to find out what happens.

I really hate cliffhangers. Sometimes I forget to read the next one. Sometimes I have a million other books to read in between, and by the time I get back to the series, I've lost the thread. And it's even more frustrating when the next book isn't out yet, or it is and I just haven't gotten to it.

So one star off. Not because the book wasn't good, but because it made me wait.

## The verdict

Four stars. It's cute, it's funny, and it made me root for the bad guy. Just don't start it unless you have book two, *Apprentice to the Villain*, ready to go.
