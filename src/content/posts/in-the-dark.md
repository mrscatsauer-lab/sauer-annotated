<aside class="note">five lines, one sentence, no capital letters. it should feel whispered.</aside>

<aside class="note">“loving in the dark” can mean secret, or grieving, or hidden. the poem never picks.</aside>

<aside class="note">the line break leaves “refuses” hanging alone for a breath before we learn what it refuses.</aside>

<div class="poem">there is something beautiful
<mark class="clue">about loving in the dark,</mark>
when the world cannot see
what the heart <mark class="clue">refuses</mark>
<mark class="clue">to let go.</mark></div>
