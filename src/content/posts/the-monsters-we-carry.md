---
title: The Monsters We Carry
description: A little girl hiding in a closet, the woman she became, and the day she finally opened the door.
date: 2026-11-09
shelf: writing-life
tags: [poetry]
draft: false
---

<div class="poem stanza">a little girl cries into the night,
<em>“help me, please.
can anybody hear me?”</em></div>

<div class="poem stanza">but no one comes.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">a mother’s cries fill the darkness,
an angry man towers above,
and monsters wear the faces
of the people we are told to love.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">she hides inside a closet,
her little body trembling,
holding her breath,
praying the darkness will swallow her
before he finds her.</div>

<div class="poem stanza"><em>will today be the day i die?</em></div>

<div class="poem stanza">outside, the streets are restless.
gangs claim corners,
bullets tear through the night,
and childhood is measured
in the distance between gunshots.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">she learns to run
before she learns to trust.
she learns that silence
sometimes means survival.</div>

<div class="poem stanza"><em>will today be the day i die?</em></div>

<div class="poem stanza">and then her mother leaves.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">no goodbye that makes sense,
no promise to return,
no voice calling her name.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">just an empty space
where a mother’s love
was supposed to live.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">and the little girl wonders
what was so wrong with her
that even her mother
could walk away.</div>

<hr />

<div class="poem stanza">years pass.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">the streets grow quiet.
the bullets disappear.
the closet door stands open.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">she is safe now.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">but no one told the monsters.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">they follow her into dreams,
crawl beneath her skin,
whisper through the silence
of an otherwise peaceful life.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">she wakes with a voiceless scream,
her heart racing,
her hands searching
for something she cannot name.</div>

<div class="poem stanza"><em>can anybody hear me?</em></div>

<div class="poem stanza">and one day,
her mother returns.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">the little girl, now grown,
waits for the missing pieces
to fall into place.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">but the emptiness remains.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">because sometimes
the person who broke your heart
cannot be the one
who teaches it to heal.</div>

<hr />

<div class="poem stanza">she builds a home,
a marriage,
a life that little girl
could never have imagined.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">and then she sees them.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">her daughter’s face.
her son’s face.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">and there it is,
the love she was never sure
could exist.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">it is real.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">and she carries it.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">she becomes the arms
she once needed,
the voice that answers,
the promise that stays.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">but even love
cannot erase every shadow.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">a slammed door
becomes a gunshot.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">a raised voice
becomes a monster.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">and just like that,
she is small again,
hiding in a closet
that no longer exists.</div>

<hr />

<div class="poem stanza">and then, years later,
she sees him.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">the monster.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">not towering,
not powerful,
not the giant
who haunted her childhood.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">just an old man,
frail and small.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">and she realizes
he hasn’t held power over her
in years.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">but the little girl
never knew.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">she was still hiding,
still trembling,
still waiting for someone
to open the door.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">but no one was coming.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">and this time,
she did not wait.</div>

<hr />

<div class="poem stanza">She fought.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">Through the fear,
through the shame,
through every night
she had lain awake
listening for his footsteps.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">She found her voice
where silence had lived.
She found her strength
in her children’s faces.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">She turned toward the closet,
toward the shadows,
toward the child
still hiding inside them.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">And she opened the door.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">“I hear you,” she whispered.
“I’ve always heard you.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">You kept us alive.
Now it’s my turn
to give us a life.”</div>

<div class="poem stanza">And the little girl who once begged
for someone to save her
has become the woman
who did.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">Once, she asked the darkness,
<em>will today be the day i die?</em></div>

<div class="poem stanza">Now she looks the monsters in the eye.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">Not today.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">Today is the day I live.</div>
