<aside class="note">her voice is always in italics. the narrator’s never is.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">a little girl cries into the night,
<em>“help me, please.
<mark class="clue">can anybody hear me?</mark>”</em></div>

<div class="poem stanza">but no one comes.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">a mother’s cries fill the darkness,
an angry man towers above,
and monsters wear the faces
of the people we are told to love.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">she hides inside a closet,
praying the darkness will swallow her
before he finds her.</div>

<aside class="note">the refrain. it comes back once more, at the very end.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza"><em><mark class="clue">will today be the day i die?</mark></em></div>

<aside class="note">the poem measures childhood instead of describing it.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">outside, the streets are restless.
gangs claim corners,
bullets tear through the night,
and childhood is measured
<mark class="clue">in the distance between gunshots.</mark></div>

<div class="poem stanza">and then her mother leaves,
leaving an empty space
where a mother’s love
was supposed to live.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">and the little girl wonders
what was so wrong with her
that even her mother
could walk away.</div>
