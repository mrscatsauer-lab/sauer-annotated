<aside class="note">every sense gets a turn: sight, smell, sound, touch.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">the darkness swallows everything.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">piles of clothes,
stained and sour,
cockroaches crawling in the corners,
tiny claws scratching
inside the walls.</div>

<aside class="note">the sounds are in italics. she has to stay silent, so the house gets to be loud.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza"><em>scratch.
scurry.
creak.</em></div>

<div class="poem stanza">she sits among the piles,
shivering,
knees pressed to her chest,
fingers digging into her legs,
numb from holding on too tight.</div>

<aside class="note">a scared child turns ordinary things into monsters. the poem never names one.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">a hanging coat becomes a figure.
<mark class="clue">every shadow has eyes.</mark>
<mark class="clue">every creak has teeth.</mark></div>

<aside class="note">the whole poem in three lines.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">she fears the darkness,
<mark class="clue">but fears more</mark>
<mark class="clue">what waits beyond the door.</mark></div>

<div class="poem stanza">a scream.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">her eyes squeeze shut.</div>

<aside class="note">one heartbeat, between two screams.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza"><em>thump. thump.
thump. thump.</em></div>

<div class="poem stanza">another scream.</div>

<aside class="note">the same word three times. she shrinks, and the poem never says how.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza"><mark class="clue">smaller.</mark>
<mark class="clue">smaller.</mark>
<mark class="clue">smaller.</mark></div>

<div class="poem stanza">perhaps she can disappear
beneath the clothes,
the dust,
the debris.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">a floorboard creaks.</div>

<aside class="note">a comma where a period should be. the held breath carries into the last lines.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza"><mark class="clue">she holds her breath,</mark></div>

<div class="poem stanza">praying for the door to open.</div>

<aside class="note">the same prayer, pointed in opposite directions.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza"><mark class="clue">praying</mark>
<mark class="clue">it never does.</mark></div>
