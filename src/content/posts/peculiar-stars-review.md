Oh my gosh. I just finished *Peculiar Stars*, and I need to talk about it.

I couldn't put it down. I finished it in two days, switching back and forth between my Kindle and the audiobook so I never had to stop.

## The setup

Callista is engaged to Darren Knight and on her way to their engagement party aboard a luxury yacht when a cyclone wrecks it. The survivors wash up on an uncharted island. And they're stuck there for eighteen months.

One of the people stranded with her is Dominic, a former Army medic.

You can probably see where this is going.

## Dominic

I love Dominic. His whole personality is taking care of people. On an island where everyone is scared, hungry, and hurt, he's the one patching people up and holding things together. That caring nature is what makes him so easy to fall for.

<aside class="note">a hero whose love language is first aid. sign me up.</aside>

## Then and now

The book switches between *then*, on the island, and *now*, after they're rescued and trying to live in a world that kept going without them. I loved that format. You know they survive, so the suspense isn't whether they make it. It's what happened out there, and what it cost them.

## The one thing that bothered me

I would never justify cheating. That's the only thing about this book that bothered me.

I didn't like Darren from the start, and the author clearly works hard to make sure you don't either. (More on that in the spoilers.) I understand why. If Darren is awful enough, it's easier to root for Callista and Dominic.

But two wrongs don't make a right. Darren being a bad guy doesn't make cheating on him okay.

## The question I can't answer

Here's what I keep coming back to, though. If you'd been stranded for eighteen months and truly believed you would never be rescued, would you do the same?

That's a hard one. Who knows what any of us would do. I'd like to think I know. I'm not sure anybody really does.

<aside class="note">book club question #1. you're welcome.</aside>

## The verdict

Five stars. I cried. I laughed. I went through every up and down with these characters. Even with the part that bothered me, it's a really good read, and I didn't want it to end.

And Darren. He cheats, he lies, and he turns out to be just a horrible person. That's the author's way of making it right, so you don't feel bad about who Callista ends up with. It mostly works. I still don't think it makes the cheating okay.
