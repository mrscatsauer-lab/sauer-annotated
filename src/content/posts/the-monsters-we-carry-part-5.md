<aside class="note">lowercase for four parts. capital letters the moment she fights.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza"><mark class="clue">She fought,</mark>
through the fear,
through the shame,
through every night
she had lain awake
listening for his footsteps.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">She turned toward the closet,
toward the shadows,
toward the child
still hiding inside them.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">And she opened the door.</div>

<aside class="note">the answer to “can anybody hear me?” from part one.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">“<mark class="clue">I hear you</mark>,” she whispered.
“<mark class="clue">I’ve always heard you.</mark></div>

<div class="poem stanza">You kept us alive.
Now it’s my turn
to give us a life.”</div>

<div class="poem stanza">And the little girl who once begged
for someone to save her
has become the woman
who did.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">Once, she asked the darkness,
<em>will today be the day i die?</em></div>

<div class="poem stanza">Now she looks the monsters in the eye.</div>

<aside class="note">lowercase “i” when she asks if she’ll die. capital “I” when she lives.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza"><mark class="clue">Not today.</mark></div>

<div class="poem stanza"><mark class="clue">Today is the day I live.</mark></div>
