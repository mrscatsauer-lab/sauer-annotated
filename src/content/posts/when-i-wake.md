<aside class="note">all lowercase on purpose. it should feel half-awake, written on the edge of the bed.</aside>

<aside class="note">the whole poem runs on light and dark. the first stanza turns a light on.</aside>

<div class="poem">sometimes i dream
of you and me,
<mark class="clue">of the dark i lived in</mark>
<mark class="clue">and the warmth you brought,</mark>
<mark class="clue">like someone leaving a light on</mark>
<mark class="clue">just for me,</mark>
of everything we were
and everything we couldn’t be.</div>

<aside class="note">the second stanza brings real light in, and it’s the wrong light.</aside>

<aside class="note">“just for me” becomes “just a dream.” same word, opposite feeling.</aside>

<div class="poem">and when i wake,
<mark class="clue">the morning light comes in,</mark>
<mark class="clue">but it isn’t yours,</mark>
and i find myself wondering
if you were ever really mine,
or if you were always
<mark class="clue">just a dream.</mark></div>
