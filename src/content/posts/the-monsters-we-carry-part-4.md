<div class="poem stanza">years later,
she sees him.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">not towering,
not the giant
who haunted her childhood.
just an old man,
frail and small.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">he hasn’t held power over her
in years.</div>

<aside class="note">the turn. the danger is gone; the child inside never got the news.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">but the little girl
<mark class="clue">never knew.</mark></div>

<div class="poem stanza">she was still hiding,
still waiting for someone
to open the door.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">but no one was coming.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">and this time,
<mark class="clue">she did not wait.</mark></div>
