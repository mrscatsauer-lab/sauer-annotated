<div class="poem">and when i look into your eyes,
i still see the shadows
of all you’ve survived.</div>

<div class="poem">perhaps you see mine, too.</div>

<div class="poem">but somewhere
behind the shadows,
i see a place
where the monsters
no longer rule,
where strength can rest,
where you reach for my hand
and walk on the outside,
and love need not be spoken
to be known.</div>

<div class="poem">i see the country roads
we drive together
toward the life
we’re building,
the oaks beneath which
we will grow old.</div>

<div class="poem">i see you.
i see me.</div>

<div class="poem">and reflected between us,
i see home.</div>

<aside class="note">for Kevin</aside>
