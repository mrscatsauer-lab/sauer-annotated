<aside class="note">vibrations, not sounds. that’s how tarantulas actually hunt.</aside>

<mark class="clue">The vibration of frenzied movements</mark> wakes me from my sleep. I’m surrounded by darkness, snuggled down in my bed of dirt and debris. The burrow is my happy place. Another vibration of hysteria entices me to move from the warmth of my underground chamber.

<aside class="note">blue light, every night, same time. a moon doesn’t do that. a tank light does.</aside>

At the small opening of my home, the darkness is interrupted by <mark class="clue">a soft blue glow, the only moon I have ever known.</mark> <mark class="clue">My hairy, black legs</mark> stretch through the dirt floor with anticipation. The fear of my struggling victim sends the thrill of the hunt through my body.

<aside class="note">eight eyes, nearly blind. we know what it is by now. the question is where.</aside>

Slowly, I inch out of the tiny doorway and peek out to survey my surroundings. <mark class="clue">My eight beady eyes can hardly see,</mark> but I know what lies ahead. Beyond my dirt cave are green dew-dropped leaves glittering like stars in the night sky.

<aside class="note">“seems.” one word doing a lot of heavy lifting.</aside>

<aside class="note">a cricket, alone, with nowhere to run. someone put him there.</aside>

My domain <mark class="clue">seems</mark> to stretch on forever, and my life’s work lies at its heart. A silken ivory web shakes with every frantic movement of <mark class="clue">the intruder.</mark> He jumps over and past my web in an attempt to flee, but his movements tell me where to find him. I am a master of the hunt.

My body tenses for a moment. Then I shoot forward through dry leaves toward him. My fangs sink deep, and warmth floods through me as he goes still. I can feel the life of the cricket slipping away. With the limp carcass dangling from my mouth, I drag my meal back into my peaceful home. Once nothing of him remains, my body slumps down into my bedding.

<aside class="note">and there it is. the moon has a switch.</aside>

Far above, beyond <mark class="clue">the glass walls I have never once tried to cross,</mark> a giant shadow leans close. A soft tap echoes through my world. <mark class="clue">Then the blue moon clicks off.</mark>

My entire being relaxes with contentment. I am grateful, and I think to myself, *Thank you for dying, so I can live another day.*
