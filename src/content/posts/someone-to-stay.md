<aside class="note">the first three stanzas are a daydream: soft, open, all imagining.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">i imagine love
as a feeling of complete trust.
no walls,
no secrets,
nothing left to fear.</div>

<aside class="note">light and monsters. the same images as the other poems on this shelf.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">where darkness is only a shadow,
<mark class="clue">and the light is something</mark>
<mark class="clue">we create between us.</mark>
where even <mark class="clue">the monsters</mark>
of my past
wouldn’t dare enter.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">i imagine the quiet moments,
the warmth of being held,
the certainty of knowing
<mark class="clue">someone will stay.</mark></div>

<aside class="note">one line, standing alone, and the whole poem turns.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza"><mark class="clue">but i grew up with another kind of love.</mark></div>

<aside class="note">the dream was soft and vague. this stanza is only hard, specific images.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">the loud kind.
doors slamming,
fists hurting,
empty promises,
and later, in my own,
me fleeing.</div>

<div class="poem stanza">the fear of being left,
the urge to leave first,
the endless questioning
of everything i thought was real.</div>

<aside class="note">“recognize” quietly sets up the ending.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">i’ve spent my life
searching for a love
i’m not sure <mark class="clue">i’d recognize.</mark></div>

<div class="poem stanza">perhaps the <mark class="clue">bedtime</mark> stories lied,
perhaps love was never meant
to feel like a fairy tale.</div>

<aside class="note">“someone will stay” comes back as “someone to stay.” the wish becomes a wait.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">or perhaps
the little girl who was left behind
is still waiting
<mark class="clue">for someone to stay.</mark></div>

<aside class="note">the last stanza turns the question around.</aside>

<div class="poem stanza">and no matter how much love
stands before her,
she’s still searching
<mark class="clue">for the one</mark>
<mark class="clue">who walked away.</mark></div>
