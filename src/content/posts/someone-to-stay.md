---
title: Someone to Stay
description: The love I imagine, the love I grew up with, and the little girl still waiting in between.
date: 2026-11-08
shelf: writing-life
tags: [poetry]
draft: false
---

<div class="poem">i imagine love
as a feeling of complete trust.
no walls,
no secrets,
nothing left to fear.</div>

<div class="poem">where darkness is only a shadow,
and the light is something
we create between us.
where even the monsters
of my past
wouldn’t dare enter.</div>

<div class="poem">i imagine the quiet moments,
the warmth of being held,
the certainty of knowing
someone will stay.</div>

<div class="poem">but i grew up with another kind of love.</div>

<div class="poem">the loud kind.
doors slamming,
fists hurting,
empty promises,
and later, in my own,
me fleeing.</div>

<div class="poem">the fear of being left,
the urge to leave first,
the endless questioning
of everything i thought was real.</div>

<div class="poem">i’ve spent my life
searching for a love
i’m not sure i’d recognize.</div>

<div class="poem">perhaps the bedtime stories lied,
perhaps love was never meant
to feel like a fairy tale.</div>

<div class="poem">or perhaps
the little girl who was left behind
is still waiting
for someone to stay.</div>

<div class="poem">and no matter how much love
stands before her,
she’s still searching
for the one
who walked away.</div>
